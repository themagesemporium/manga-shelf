Back up or bring in a collection

Save a backup of your whole shelf, or bring books in from a backup, a list from another app, or a file the shop sends you. Imports add to what you have; nothing is removed.

Every collection is checked daily for spelling and consistency, and matched to the shop’s series so the right covers and info show up. If something can’t be matched, you’ll see ⚠️ on that series and in the list below, where you can fix it or leave us a note.

📸 Send us photos instead

Big collection? Take photos of your shelves with the spines readable and email them to us. We’ll add every book to your shelf for you.

  1. Log in first, so we know which account to fill.
  2. Tap the button below. It opens an email to us.
  3. Attach your photos (as many emails as you need) and send.

Photos have to be processed by hand, so it can take a few days. You’ll get an email when your shelf is ready.
